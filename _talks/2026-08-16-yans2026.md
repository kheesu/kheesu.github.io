Poster presentation on grounding word sense induction in vision: building visual anchors from ImageNet images and using them to disambiguate word senses, comparing text-only and multimodal clustering across SemCor, DWUG EN, and SemEval benchmarks. Joint work with Zhidong Ling, Hajime Kiyama, and Prof. Mamoru Komachi (Komachi Lab, Hitotsubashi University).
